Add floating and inline social share buttons with click-to-copy link, share counts and Open Graph / Twitter meta support.

== Description ==

Social Share Master makes it easy to add beautiful share buttons to your WordPress site.

Features:

* Floating sidebar and inline content buttons.
* Networks: Facebook, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Pinterest, Email, Copy Link.
* Open Graph and Twitter Card meta tags.
* Per post-type control.
* Multiple button styles: rounded, square, minimal.
* Left or right floating position.

== Installation ==

1. Upload `social-share-master` to `wp-content/plugins/`.
2. Activate the plugin.
3. Go to **Tools > Social Share Master** to choose networks and post types.
